## Public copy

### Artist bio

Brrainz makes strange, atmospheric soundtracks for worlds worth surviving.
Zombieland - Calm and Zombieland - Tense collect the music from the Zombieland
RimWorld mod: quiet horizons, uneasy machinery, sudden danger, and the long
walk after the outbreak.

### Zombieland - Calm description

The quieter side of the Zombieland soundtrack: distant colonies, weathered
horizons, late-night machines, and a little room to breathe.

### Zombieland - Tense description

The restless side of the Zombieland soundtrack: alarms, shadows, infected
ground, and the feeling that something is moving just outside the light.

### Launch note

The Zombieland soundtrack is now available to listen to outside the game.
Start with Zombieland - Calm for the long nights, then Zombieland - Tense when
the colony stops being quiet.
