Zombieland turns RimWorld into a zombie apocalypse.

Zombies crawl out of the ground, gather in hordes, follow noise and scent, bash doors, eat the wounded, and become a real threat when your colony is already busy with something else.

What it adds:

- configurable zombie hordes
- infection through zombie bites
- corpses that can rise again
- special zombies with different threats
- contamination as a slow late-game danger
- tools such as serum, thumpers, shockers, and chainsaws
- many settings for how dangerous the apocalypse should be

It is chaotic by design. If you ignore the undead for too long, the map will remind you.
